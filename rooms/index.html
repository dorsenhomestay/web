Get in touch
How do I book a room?

Choose a room and send an enquiry or call us directly. We’ll confirm availability, the final rate, and the arrangements for your stay.

What is the difference between the room types?

Our Deluxe Rooms have a king bed; Semi Deluxe Rooms have a queen bed. Every listed room accommodates two guests and has an attached verandah. Browse the photographs to find your favourite.

Can you help with airport transfers and sightseeing?

Yes. Contact our travel partner, Romel Compass, to discuss airport pickup and drop-off, sightseeing, and accommodation arrangements.

What if my dates or plans are flexible?

That’s absolutely fine. Leave the dates blank in your enquiry and tell us what you have in mind. We can discuss room options and availability with you.
